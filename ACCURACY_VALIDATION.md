## 1. Empirical Grounding & Provenance Disclosure

### 1.1 Synthetic Pipeline Verification vs Empirical Human Ground Truth
Earlier project drafts referenced a 144-window evaluation reporting $77.8\%$ exact tier agreement and $100\%$ one-tier tolerance. An exhaustive data provenance audit (`EVIDENCE_AUDIT.md`) established that those 144 data points in `shared_state/ema_validation_trials.json` were synthetically generated by `scripts/run_ema_validation.py` using statistical distributions (`np.random.normal` and `random.choices`) to verify the evaluation math, pipeline parsing, and confusion matrix rendering.

In accordance with strict academic integrity:
1. **The synthetic numbers are NOT presented as empirical human ground truth.**
2. **Empirical EMA Accuracy is formally classified as: `NOT COLLECTED / REQUIRES HUMAN STUDY`.**
3. A complete, turnkey collection harness and standardized human study protocol have been established in `scripts/ema_collector.py` and `docs/EMA_RECOLLECTION_KIT.md` for execution prior to final viva.

### 1.2 Model Quantization Fidelity vs Human Ground Truth
Model compression fidelity (quantization agreement) and human affective agreement are two distinct concepts:
- **Model Quantization Agreement:** Measures how closely a quantized model (e.g. Q4_0) matches the floating-point predictions (FP16) of the teacher model on identical prompt inputs. This was previously benchmarked in literature on other models but is **`NOT MEASURED`** for Gemma 4 E2B on this machine because unquantized FP16 weights were not stored locally.
- **Human Ground-Truth Agreement:** Measures how accurately the system's inferred affective tier matches the participant's subjective, momentary self-reported psychological state collected via Ecological Momentary Assessment (EMA).

## 2. Standardized Experimental Protocol (Human Study Kit)

The protocol below is authored and packaged in `docs/EMA_RECOLLECTION_KIT.md` for execution by the student team.

### 2.1 Participant Sample & Ethics
- **Cohort Size:** Minimum $N \ge 8$ human participants (recommended: 4 project developers + 4 external peer student volunteers) to assess agreement across both trained and untrained subjects.
- **Ethics & Privacy:** Complete informed consent signed prior to participation; participants may withdraw at any time.
- **Local Boundary:** All feature extraction and reasoning occur 100% locally on `127.0.0.1`. No video frames, audio recordings, or participant embeddings are transmitted to any external server or saved to persistent disk. Media is processed ephemerally in RAM and immediately freed.

### 2.2 Experimental Tasks & Protocol (20-Minute Battery)
Each participant completes four 5-minute capture windows:

| Condition ID | Activity Description | Active Modalities | Expected Ground Truth |
|:---|:---|:---:|:---:|
| **Condition 1 (Baseline)** | Quiet technical reading and code documentation review. Silent, natural facial posture. | Video (Facial AUs) | **Tier 0** (Composite Score 3–5) |
| **Condition 2 (Conversation)** | Active spoken interaction; explaining project architecture aloud to Parlor. | Video + Audio | **Tier 0 / Tier 1** (Score 3–10) |
| **Condition 3 (Stressor)** | High-speed mental arithmetic and timed Stroop color-word interference task under strict 60s timer. | Video + Audio | **Tier 2** (Score 11–15) |
| **Condition 4 (Fallback)** | Dimmed ambient lighting (<50 lux) or camera occluded during speech. | Audio-Only | **Tier 1 / Tier 2** (Score 6–15) |

### 2.3 The 3-Item EMA Survey Instrument
Administered at the conclusion of each capture window via `python scripts/ema_collector.py` (1–5 Likert scale):
1. *"How tense, anxious, or stressed did you feel during the last capture window?"* (1: Calm $\rightarrow$ 5: Extremely Tense)
2. *"How overwhelmed did you feel by your current activity?"* (1: In Control $\rightarrow$ 5: Overwhelmed)
3. *"Did you notice physical tension (brow furrowing, jaw clenching, rapid breathing)?"* (1: None $\rightarrow$ 5: Severe)

**Scoring Schema:**
$$\text{Composite EMA Score} = Q_1 + Q_2 + Q_3 \quad (\text{Range: } 3 \text{ to } 15)$$
- **Tier 0 (Low Stress / Baseline):** Composite Score `3 – 5`
- **Tier 1 (Moderate Stress / Elevated Tension):** Composite Score `6 – 10`
- **Tier 2 (High Stress / Acute Tension):** Composite Score `11 – 15`

## 4. Key Limitations & Clinical Boundary Disclosure

1. **Non-Clinical Boundary:** Parlor is strictly an assistive productivity tool designed to estimate stress-related behavioral signals and suggest ergonomic breaks. It is **NOT** clinically validated and does **NOT** diagnose anxiety, clinical depression, or medical disorders.
2. **Participant Demographics & Small N:** Peer evaluation cohorts consist of undergraduate engineering students. Baseline facial mobility and vocal characteristics vary across demographics, accents, and age groups, which require broader population studies.
3. **EMA Subjectivity & Recall Lag:** Self-report ratings are inherently subjective and influenced by personal stress thresholds. Retrospective reporting over 5-minute windows may smooth over momentary peak tension spikes.
4. **Lighting & Acoustic Sensitivity:** While graceful audio fallback is implemented, single-modality tracking exhibits wider confidence bounds than concurrent multimodal capture.
5. **GPU Sharing Contention:** Running affective reasoning concurrently with speech adds $+0.114\text{s}$ mean latency to voice TTFA on a single RTX 4050 GPU (1.789s loop off vs 1.903s loop on mitigated in our 30-turn alternating benchmark), which is mitigated by deferring wellness SLM cycles during active conversation turns. Furthermore, zero-signal windows (`"insufficient signal"`) are strictly isolated and excluded from EMA ground-truth analysis.
