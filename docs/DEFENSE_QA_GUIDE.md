## 1. Core Examination Questions and Defense Answers

### Q1: "Why use a Small Language Model (SLM) for stress detection instead of simple statistical thresholds?"
**Answer:**  
"Static numeric thresholds fail in real-world human interaction because affective expression is non-linear and context-dependent. For example, when a user speaks enthusiastically or laughs during a meeting, their vocal pitch rises ($\Delta F0 > 30\text{ Hz}$), which trips a fixed threshold and triggers an irritating false-positive stress alarm. In contrast, our SLM (Gemma 4 E2B IT) performs qualitative cross-modal pattern reasoning: when presented with elevated vocal pitch alongside completely relaxed facial musculature (AU04 = 0.05), the model evaluates the conflict, outputs Tier 1 instead of Tier 2, and explicitly reasons that vocal tension without facial contraction indicates animated conversation rather than distress. Furthermore, the SLM produces explainable, human-readable rationales citing the contributing biological markers, providing the transparency required for user trust."

### Q3: "How is privacy guaranteed? How do we know biometric data isn't leaked?"
**Answer:**  
"Privacy is guaranteed by architectural design, not merely by policy:
1. **Network Boundary Isolation:** All services bind exclusively to loopback addresses (`127.0.0.1:8000` for FastAPI and `127.0.0.1:8081` for `llama-server`). A live network socket packet audit (`netstat -ano`) during active video and audio streaming verified **0.00 KB** of outbound network transmission.
2. **Standard Operation Boundary:** The system operates with no outbound network traffic during standard operation; model weights are downloaded once during initial setup; the optional research mode is the only feature capable of making external API calls.
3. **Local Asset Vendoring:** Silero VAD and ONNX runtime WebAssembly assets are vendored locally in `src/parlor/web/static/vad/`, completely removing third-party CDN connections (`cdn.jsdelivr.net`).
4. **Data Ephemerality:** Raw video frames and microphone audio buffers are processed in volatile memory and immediately discarded. Only anonymized scalar feature vectors (e.g., brow contraction ratios and pitch delta) and generated JSON reasoning strings are saved in local SQLite (`wellness_history.db`). No raw facial images or audio recordings are ever stored or transmitted."

### Q4: "What happens when the model goes offline or sensor signals are poor?"
**Answer:**  
"The system incorporates three layers of graceful degradation:
1. **Poor Visual Signal / Low Light:** If the camera is covered or lighting is insufficient, MediaPipe FaceMesh flags `face_detected: False`. The pipeline gracefully transitions to audio-only mode, evaluating vocal acoustic DSP features alone with conservative confidence scaling.
2. **Zero Signal (Silent and Unseen):** When both camera and mic lack signal, the system stays honest: it records and emits `stress_level = "insufficient signal"`, `numeric_score = -1.0`, and `confidence_score = 0.0`, suppressing all intervention firing and isolating zero-signal windows from EMA ground-truth analysis.
3. **SLM Process Crash (`llama-server` killed):** If `llama-server` terminates mid-session, the conversational voice pipeline catches `LLAMAServerError`, sends an actionable error message to the UI, and releases the client immediately without hanging. Concurrently, the wellness service engages exponential backoff, preserves local features in SQLite, and automatically reconnects once `llama-server` restarts."

### Q5: "Why Gemma 4 E2B IT, and why this specific quantization format?"
**Answer:**  
"Selecting Gemma 4 E2B IT at `Q4_0` quantization was driven by strict edge hardware budgets:
- **Footprint:** The uncompressed 16-bit model requires $>16\text{ GB}$ VRAM, which is impossible on consumer laptops. Quantizing to `Q4_0` reduces the model size to **3.35 GB** (with a 986 MB multimodal projector), enabling complete offload into our laptop's 6 GB RTX 4050 GPU VRAM with a peak session allocation of **3.90 GB VRAM**.
- **Latency:** Gemma 4 E2B executes qualitative structured reasoning in **2.14 s mean / 2.45 s p95** on GPU (pooled across all prompt types: **2.23 s mean / 2.49 s p95 / 4.32 s max**), easily fitting within our 5.0-second rolling observation window budget while leaving a 0.68-second safety margin even on the worst observed cross-modal conflict deliberation.
- **Multimodal Support:** The companion `mmproj` weights allow multimodal image and audio projection directly into the SLM when needed."

### Q6: "How does Parlor compare to biometric wearables and manual self-report logging?"
**Answer:**  
"Parlor offers significant advantages across user friction, privacy, and explainability:
1. **Compared to Wearables (Smartwatches / Chest Straps):** Wearables cost \$200–\$500, require daily charging, and cause skin discomfort, leading to high abandonment rates. More importantly, wearables track autonomic arousal (HRV/GSR) but cannot observe *context* or explain *why* stress occurred. Parlor requires zero extra hardware, operates passively through standard laptop sensors, and provides natural-language explainability.
2. **Compared to Manual Logging Apps:** Mobile mood-tracking apps demand active user disruption, resulting in substantial user attrition within early weeks (Baumel et al., 2019; Eysenbach, 2005). Parlor is completely passive, continuously evaluating baseline deviations in the background without interrupting deep work."

### Q7: "What are the ethical risks regarding false alarms, over-reliance, and demographic bias across skin tone, lighting, or glasses?"
**Answer:**  
"We have addressed these ethical risks through deliberate algorithmic constraints:
1. **Alert Fatigue & False Alarms:** We implement a multi-cycle hysteresis trigger policy requiring sustained elevated tension (e.g., 3 consecutive cycles in default Balanced mode with 300s cooldown, 4 cycles / 450s in Conservative, 2 cycles / 180s in Sensitive) before an alert fires, combined with user snooze and mute controls.
2. **Over-Reliance / Medical Boundary:** Parlor is clearly scoped as an occupational wellness tool to encourage micro-breaks; it is explicitly not a diagnostic tool and contains disclaimer notices preventing over-reliance.
3. **Demographic Equity & Optical Occlusions:** Demographic equity and glasses effects have not been formally evaluated across diverse demographic populations on our system. MediaPipe FaceMesh uses geometric landmark distance ratios normalized to facial anchor points rather than raw pixel intensities, and our architecture gracefully falls back to vocal acoustics when facial landmarks cannot be extracted, but comprehensive multi-cohort demographic validation remains an essential requirement for future human clinical trials."
