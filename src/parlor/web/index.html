    Purpose: You are invited to participate in an evaluation of on-device affective wellness detection. The system estimates stress-related biometric markers (facial action units and vocal prosody) in real time.

    Privacy & Local Storage: All computation and logging occur strictly on this local computer in local files (study_data/). Nothing is transmitted to external servers.
